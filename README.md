***How do I approach a problem as a computer scientist?***
- Computer scientists face various problems when developing AI model. AI model often users personal data as training data. GRPR, The General Data Protection Regulation, defines principles with personal data. This provides benchmark for any company that uses personal data for AI model. A company needs to be transparent in the handling of data, use it as necessary not to excess, allow owners of data for control, audit the process, and finally, personal data needs to be securely stored.

***What are my ethical responsibilities to the end user and the organization?***
- Ethical responsibilities exist with AI model. AI model should be fair, transparent, harmless and free of bias. Companies need to make sure training data, method, and outcome follow the principles defined by GDPR. End users also agree with terms provided by companies and must not abuse AI model. Once such incident is photoshopping a celebrity's face into a photo, creating false illusion to mislead or hurt their reputation. Such incidents need to be taken seriously and be prevented at all cost.
